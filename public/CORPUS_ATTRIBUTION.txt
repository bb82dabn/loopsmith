LoopSmith training corpus attribution
=====================================

LoopSmith uses only aggregate statistics derived offline from the following
licensed MIDI source. No source MIDI, audio, melody, chord sequence, rhythm
template, or arrangement is included in LoopSmith or its generated exports.

Generic 8-bit JRPG Soundtrack
Creator: AVGVSTA
Source: https://opengameart.org/content/generic-8-bit-jrpg-soundtrack
Selected license: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International
License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
Use: aggregate interval, duration, onset, harmonic-motion, density, and
arrangement statistics across 25 MIDI files.
